Privacy Policy — Exertrack

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Exertrack ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Who we are

Exertrack is a workout tracking application developed by Dorian Black.

For any privacy-related questions, you can contact us at: exertrack@dorianblack.net

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data stored locally on your device

The App stores the following data in a local database on your device:

  • Workout plans, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, notes, and day comments
  • Workout history and statistics
  • App settings, preferences, and diagnostic error logs

This data remains on your device unless you use the backup or sharing features described below. If you enable automatic backup, the App may subsequently upload new database copies without a separate action each time.

2.2 Google Account information (optional)

If you choose to connect your Google Account, the App receives:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Authentication tokens used to access the Google services you authorize

Connecting a Google Account is optional: you can record and view workouts without it. Authentication tokens are stored using the device's secure storage.

2.3 Google Drive (optional)

If you start a manual backup or enable automatic backup, the App uploads a copy of its local database to the connected account's Google Drive. That copy may include workout data, day comments, settings, and diagnostic error logs. It is stored in your Google Drive and subject to your Google Drive access settings. Exertrack does not operate a server that receives the backup.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not operate a server that receives your workout data
  • We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or app usage telemetry
  • We do not sell or rent your data
  • We do not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone

4. Sharing features

The App allows you to voluntarily share workout data, for example by exporting a workout file or QR code to send to someone. Sharing is initiated by you. Shared workouts contain exercises and sets, not day comments or the full database. The recipient and any sharing service you choose can access the data you send.

5. Third-party services

The App integrates with the following third-party services if you choose to connect Google Drive:

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Local data: you can delete it by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.
  • Google Drive backups: you can delete the backup from the App's Settings page or directly from your Google Drive. Disconnecting Google does not delete an existing backup.
  • Google access: you can disconnect in the App's Settings page or revoke access at Google Account permissions.

The App does not create an Exertrack account.

7. Security

Your local data is protected by your device's security mechanisms. Authentication tokens are stored using platform secure storage. Communication with Google services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
